**Assignment Title:** Visual Prompt Studio 1.2

**Prompt:** Create a promotional album cover for an acoustic folk band showing an open grass landscape with trees, mountains and an acoustic guitar leaning on a tree. The acoustic guitar and tree should be bigger to show they are closer to the viewers eye and to show the importance as they are the main focal point. Use soft natural lighting to create a calm and peaceful mood. The bright natural light should shine over the guitar and the trees showing lighter values compared to the shadows beneath the trees and guitar showing the darker values. There should be a grass path leading the viewers eye towards the mountains and sunset in the background. The trees along the path should be getting smaller and eventually vanishing in the background creating a sense of depth. The background should be lighter compared to the guitar to show distance. The sunset should include soft yellow and orange tones helping give the viewer a sense of time passing in the day. Create unity by keeping a soft color pallete throughout the album. The trees and grass should have soft green tones and the guitar soft brown tones. Add texture through rough tree bark and rocky mountains to add variety into the album. Also show asymetrical balance by placing the guitar on one side of the album, while the open landscape and sunset fills the other side.

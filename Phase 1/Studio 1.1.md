**Assignment Title:** Visual Prompt Studio 1.1
**Prompt:** Create a promotional sci-fi film poster that features a futuristic city bazaar with a robot buying from a local human vendor. The robot and the vendors should both be bigger compared to the rest of the poster making them the main focal point. The robot should have a smooth heavy armor and the vendor rough worn out clothing. Use a directional line to create a path covered with small, lightweight pebbles going towards the background of the poster. This path should move the viewers eye towards the background of the bazaar. The background should have solid buildings made up of large rectangular and triangular shapes. Also include irregular lines through tangled hanging wires and crooked neon market signs. The neon signs should contrast with the darkness of the dark night sky. The robot and the city bazaar should be illuminated by the bright neon signs creating a postive space implying the busyness of the bazaar. While the dark background is the negative space showing that it is calmer compared to the bazaar.
**Vocabulary:** Lines: A line is something that an artist uses to direct the viewers eye and can be incorporated in different ways to create different things. Shape: Shapes are lines that create 2 dimensional or 3 dimensional items. Texture: Texture is how something feels or looks. We can imply how something will feel by looking at it or touching it. Volume: Volume is the space that fills up an object or place. Contrast: Contrast is using different elements to change how something looks.
